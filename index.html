About the school

Adv. Haroon Solkar High School was established in 1977 and is run as a privately managed, government-aided school. It stands in the rural Mangaon block of Raigad district, Maharashtra, under the local jurisdiction of Sai.

The school teaches from Class 5 through Class 12 and includes an attached pre-primary section. Urdu is the main language of instruction, and boys and girls study together.
