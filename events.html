School Events 2026

Highlights, celebrations, and student activities organized at Adv. Haroon Solkar High School during the 2026 academic year.

January 26, 2026

Republic Day Celebration

Flag hoisting ceremony, march-past, and patriotic songs performed by students from Classes 5 to 12.

February 28, 2026

National Science Exhibition

Students demonstrated working models on renewable energy, water conservation, and local agriculture.

August 15, 2026

Independence Day

Morning assembly, speeches in Urdu and Marathi, and cultural presentations attended by parents and SMC members.

September 5, 2026

Teachers’ Day

Senior students organized a special assembly, skits, and felicitation ceremony to honour all 12 faculty members.

October 15, 2026

Vachan Pr प्रेरणा Din (Reading Day)

Book reading sessions and essay competitions utilizing the school library’s collection of 1,835 books.

November 2026

Annual Sports Day

Track races, kabaddi, kho-kho, and relay events held on the school playground across junior and senior divisions.
